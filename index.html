About

Turning complex problems into experiences people understand.

With over 10 years of experience combining brand strategy, interaction design, and visual design to build scalable products that balance user needs with business goals.

I'm a Senior Analyst in Experience Design at GrowthOps, delivering UX and UI for well-known regional brands. I run research, stakeholder workshops and user interviews, and build the design systems that keep products consistent.

I use AI tools, including Claude, for competitive research, A/B test variations and automating repetitive Figma work, so more of my time goes into design decisions.

I'm open to remote roles and freelance projects, and I'm happy to share my full portfolio with hiring teams on request.
