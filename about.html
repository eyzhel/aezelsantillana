I design digital products and brands that turn complex problems into simple, human experiences.

I'm a senior UI/UX designer based in Manila, with over 10 years in design. I work across brand strategy, interaction design and visual design, and I build products that balance what users need with what the business wants.

I also think like a creative strategist and a brand designer, and I vibe-code: I use AI tools like Claude to build working prototypes, so ideas get tested in a browser, not just in Figma.

From content to interfaces

My path into design started with content. As a web content editor at Intership Navigation, I planned, created and published e-learning content and layouts for maritime training courses for seafarers. That was my first lesson in making complex information easy to follow.

I studied Computer Science at Colegio de San Juan de Letran, then earned a Web Design Certificate from First Academy of Computer Arts and a Front-End Development Certificate from De La Salle University. Knowing how things get built still shapes how I design them.

First experiences

At InnoVantage, I worked as a graphic designer on web interfaces and marketing assets for client and internal products, with layout work and some light front-end coding.

At Freelancer.com, I worked in-house as a UI/UX designer. I designed sitemaps, wireframes and interactive prototypes for the company's web and desktop applications and other digital products, along with brand assets and marketing collateral.

From 2018, I worked as a freelance brand strategist and UI/UX designer on Upwork, for clients around the world. I turned brand strategy and positioning into visual identities and UI systems for websites and apps, and I owned every stage: research, wireframing, visual design and client communication.

Today

Today I'm a Senior Analyst, Experience Design at GrowthOps. I deliver UX and interface design for well-known regional brands across banking, aviation, e-commerce and insurance.

I build and maintain design systems, run stakeholder workshops and lead user interviews across web and app products. I also brought AI tools, including Claude, into our design workflow for competitive research, A/B test variations and automating repetitive Figma work, so more of my time goes into design decisions.

Some of that work is under confidentiality. I'm happy to walk through it in an interview.
